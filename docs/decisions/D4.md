# D4 - Marking and reviewing the shipped career packs

## Decision needed

How should Stranote identify the unreviewed accountant, nurse, software-developer, and oracle-ebs-functional-financials packs, and who should review them?

## Context

Playbook Lesson 4 says packs for careers the owner does not practise need a real practitioner reviewer before release, and unconfirmed content should be marked TODO(practitioner). These four packs are already shipped and have no practitioner-review marker. The C3 note says to keep them; the decision is how to make their status clear and close the review gap.

The pack-builder skill uses x-unconfirmed for content the builder added beyond what the requester said. That does not mean an independent practitioner has reviewed the pack. Reusing it as the review signal could confuse those two states. The Spec 001 pack contract permits top-level x-* extension fields, while an unprefixed review field would need a contract and validator change.

## Options

### A. Reuse x-unconfirmed

Add a list of unconfirmed domain claims to each pack and show an onboarding warning when the list is non-empty. The owner recruits one practitioner for each career pack.

- **Cost:** Lowest contract cost; requires reviewing each pack to identify and list claims, then wiring the list into onboarding.
- **Risk:** The field describes content provenance, not review status. An empty list could be mistaken for a reviewed pack, and it cannot say who reviewed which version.

### B. Add an x-review block and keep the packs available with a warning

Add x-review metadata to each pack with a status (unreviewed, in-review, or reviewed), reviewer role, review date, reviewed pack version, and scope. Onboarding shows "Not yet reviewed by a practitioner" until the current version is reviewed. The owner recruits one qualified, independent practitioner for each career; one person may review multiple packs only if qualified in each domain. Store the reviewer's role in the public pack, not a personal name.

- **Cost:** Moderate: pass the extension through pack metadata and add the onboarding warning; review each pack and recruit practitioners.
- **Risk:** A review can become stale after content changes. Tie approval to the pack version and avoid wording that implies certification.

### C. Keep the files, but hide each pack from role selection until it is reviewed

The owner recruits a practitioner for each career, and the pack becomes selectable only after that practitioner reviews it.

- **Cost:** Requires a role-selection gate and practitioner reviews before users can choose these careers.
- **Risk:** Users in these careers lose access to the packs while review is pending; without committed reviewers, that could last indefinitely.

## Recommendation

Choose **B**. It separates independent review status from x-unconfirmed, makes the reviewed version and reviewer role visible, and lets existing users keep the packs with a plain warning. Until a practitioner reviews a specific version, leave its status unreviewed and keep the warning. Do not describe a review as certification.

For reviewers, have the owner recruit an independent working practitioner for each career: accounting, nursing, software development, and Oracle EBS Financials functional consulting. Each reviewer should check the domain terms, workflow examples, checklists, and claims in the version being reviewed. The owner should confirm the reviewer for each pack before implementation is treated as complete.

## Owner response

Reply D4: A, D4: B, or D4: C. For B, confirm the reviewer-recruitment approach and any known candidates.

## What this unblocks

After the owner chooses, an agent can update the four pack files and onboarding. Under B, the implementation also needs tests for metadata propagation and the warning before and after review.
