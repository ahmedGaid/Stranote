# Purchase request approvals - process improvement (fictional sample)

- Old way: email chains; requests got lost.
- New flow in Power Automate: form -> manager approval -> finance approval -> notification in Slack.
- Requests tracked in Jira with one ticket per request; weekly Kanban review.
- Results dashboard in Power BI: average approval time dropped from 6 days to 2.
- Written guide published in Confluence.
