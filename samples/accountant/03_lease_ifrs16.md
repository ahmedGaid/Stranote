# New warehouse lease - IFRS 16 working (fictional sample)

- 5 year lease, monthly rent 4,000, discount rate 6% (incremental borrowing rate).
- Recognised a right-of-use asset and a lease liability at the present value of payments.
- Monthly: interest on the lease liability, payment reduces it, and amortisation of the right-of-use asset.
- Old standard IAS 17 treated it as an operating lease; the comparison was added to the notes.
- Impairment review: none needed this year.
- Deferred tax on the difference between the tax and book value of the asset (IAS 12).
